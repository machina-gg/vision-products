# Getting Started with VisionFocus

Welcome to VisionFocus! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to start blocking distracting websites, running focus timers, and setting your daily vision — all within minutes.

## Installation

1. Visit the Chrome Web Store (coming soon)
2. Click **Add to Chrome**
3. Accept the required permissions when prompted
4. The VisionFocus icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar

> **Note:** VisionFocus requires permissions to manage website access (for blocking), store data locally (for your blocklist, timers, and vision statements), and send browser notifications (for timer alerts). No personal data is collected or transmitted externally.

## Your First Steps

### 1. Block a Distracting Website

One of the most powerful features of VisionFocus is the ability to block sites that pull your attention away from what matters.

1. Click the VisionFocus icon in the Chrome toolbar
2. Open the **Block** tab
3. Enter the URL you want to block (e.g., `youtube.com`, `twitter.com`)
4. Click **Add to Blocklist**

The site is blocked immediately. The next time you (or your browser) tries to visit it, you'll see your personal vision statement instead of the page.

### 2. Set a Focus Timer

Use the built-in timer to structure your work sessions and give yourself clear start and end points for deep focus.

1. Click the VisionFocus icon in the toolbar
2. Open the **Timer** tab
3. Choose your focus duration (e.g., 25 minutes)
4. Click **Start Timer**

When the session ends, you'll receive a browser notification prompting you to take a break. This pairs well with the [Pomodoro Technique](/en/docs/vision-focus/timers/).

### 3. Create Your Daily Vision Statement

Your vision statement is a short personal message that appears every time you try to access a blocked site. It reminds you why you're staying focused.

1. Click the VisionFocus icon
2. Open the **Vision** tab
3. Type your vision statement in the text field
4. Click **Save Vision**

From now on, every block page will display your statement front and center, helping you reconnect with your priorities before making an impulsive decision.

### Premium Features ($1.99/month)

You can upgrade at any time from the VisionFocus popup by clicking **Upgrade to Premium**.

## How the Block Page Works

When you visit a blocked site, VisionFocus replaces the page with a custom block screen that shows:

- Your **vision statement** (large and prominent)
- The **blocked URL** so you know which site triggered it
- Remaining **daily time limit** (if you set a time limit instead of a full block)
- Number of times you've **bypassed the block** today (for self-accountability)

This pause gives you a moment to reconsider whether that visit is truly necessary.
