# Vision Statements

A vision statement is a short personal message that appears on the block page every time you try to visit a blocked website. It's a moment of intentional pause — a reminder of what you're working toward and why that matters more than whatever you were about to do.

## What Is a Vision Statement?

A vision statement is a brief piece of text you write yourself. It might be:

- Your goal for today
- The reason you're staying focused right now
- A reminder of something you're working toward long-term
- An intention you've set for how you want to spend your time and energy

When a blocked site intercepts your navigation, your vision statement appears large at the top of the page — impossible to miss. That moment of recognition is the core mechanism that makes VisionFocus effective.

## Creating Your First Vision Statement

1. Click the VisionFocus icon in the Chrome toolbar
2. Open the **Vision** tab
3. Type your vision statement in the text field
4. Click **Save Vision**

That's it. Your statement will now appear on every block page. You can change it at any time — many users update it daily or at the start of each work session.

### Examples to Inspire You

Here are some examples of effective vision statements:

- "I'm finishing the client proposal today so I can fully disconnect this weekend."
- "Every focused hour I put in now is building the business I want."
- "I choose depth over distraction."
- "Today: complete the report, prep for tomorrow's meeting, leave on time."
- "My attention is my most valuable resource — I'm choosing where it goes."

Notice that the best ones are personal, specific, and connect to a genuine motivation. Generic statements like "be productive" tend to be less effective.

## Editing Your Vision Statement

Your vision statement isn't permanent — update it whenever your priorities shift.

1. Open VisionFocus and go to the **Vision** tab
2. Edit the text in the input field
3. Click **Save Vision**

Changes take effect immediately. The next time a block page appears, your updated statement will be shown.

## How It Appears on the Block Page

When VisionFocus intercepts a navigation to a blocked site, the block page displays:

1. **Your vision statement** — large and prominently at the top
2. **The blocked URL** — so you know which site triggered the block
3. **Options to continue** — a back button and (if configured) a bypass button

This layout is designed to create a deliberate pause. Before you can act, you have to see your own words. That brief moment of reflection is often enough to redirect your behavior.

## Writing an Effective Vision Statement

### Write in first person

Statements that use "I" feel more personal and action-oriented. Compare:

- Weak: "Be more productive today"
- Strong: "I'm making real progress on the product launch today"

### Be specific

Vague intentions are easy to ignore. Concrete ones create urgency and clarity:

- Weak: "Work on my project"
- Strong: "I'm finishing the landing page copy before 3pm"

### Connect to your deeper why

The more meaningful the reason, the more motivating the reminder. Think beyond the task itself:

- Surface level: "I need to finish this report"
- Deeper: "I'm delivering excellent work because I want to grow into the senior role"

### Use present or near-future tense

Frame your statement as something happening now or very soon. This makes it feel immediate rather than abstract:

- "I am focused and making progress"
- "By end of day, I'll have completed the first draft"

### Keep it short enough to read in under 10 seconds

Aim for 1–3 sentences. If it takes more than 10 seconds to read, it loses its impact as a quick reminder.

## Multiple Vision Statements (Premium)

Premium users can create and manage multiple vision statements for different contexts — work, study, personal projects, or time of day.

### What you can do with multiple statements

- Create presets for different situations (e.g., "Morning Deep Work", "Evening Reading", "Weekend Projects")
- Switch between them manually as your context changes
- Schedule statements to activate automatically based on time of day or day of the week

Access preset management and scheduling from the **Vision** tab.

### Example presets

| Preset | Statement |
|---|---|
| Work | "I'm doing my best work today and delivering real value." |
| Study | "Every hour I invest in learning compounds into expertise." |
| Personal | "I'm investing in my health and relationships — the things that matter most." |
| Evening | "I'm winding down well so tomorrow I can do it all again." |

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of Vision Statements

### Refresh it daily

Take 60 seconds each morning to update your statement to match your priorities for that specific day. A stale statement loses its power quickly.

### Test it before you need it

After saving a new statement, deliberately visit a blocked site to see how it reads. Does it resonate? Does it make you want to go back to work? If not, rewrite it.

### Pair it with your timer

Set your vision statement before starting a [focus timer](/en/docs/vision-focus/timers/). This creates a ritual: set the intention, start the timer, begin the work.

### Review at end of week

At the end of each week, look back at the statements you used. Which ones were most effective? What does that tell you about what actually motivates you?
