# Timers

VisionFocus includes a built-in focus timer designed to help you work in structured intervals, stay accountable to your time, and build a sustainable deep-work habit. Whether you follow the Pomodoro Technique or prefer longer uninterrupted blocks, the timer adapts to your style.

## Starting a Focus Timer

### Basic Setup

1. Click the VisionFocus icon in the Chrome toolbar
2. Open the **Timer** tab
3. Set your desired focus duration (e.g., 25 minutes)
4. Click **Start Timer**

The countdown begins immediately. When the session ends, you'll receive a browser notification even if the VisionFocus popup is closed.

### Choosing the Right Duration

Different types of work call for different session lengths:

| Session Type | Duration | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Short burst | 15–20 minutes | Small tasks, email triage, quick reviews |
| Standard Pomodoro | 25 minutes | Most focused work tasks |
| Deep work | 45–90 minutes | Complex problems, writing, coding |

Start with 25 minutes if you're new to structured focus. You can always increase as your concentration builds.

## Using the Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro Technique breaks work into focused intervals separated by short breaks. Research suggests this rhythm prevents mental fatigue and maintains concentration better than working for hours without stopping.

### The Classic Pomodoro Cycle

1. **Work session**: Set the timer for 25 minutes and focus on a single task
2. **Short break**: Take a 5-minute break when the timer ends
3. **Repeat**: Complete 4 work sessions
4. **Long break**: After 4 sessions, take a 15–30 minute break before the next cycle

### Running Pomodoro Sessions in VisionFocus

1. Set the timer to **25 minutes**
2. Click **Start Timer** and begin your task
3. When notified, manually start a 5-minute break timer
4. After the break, reset and begin your next session
5. Track your completed sessions manually or in a notebook

**Pro tip:** Enable website blocking during work sessions for maximum focus. Any site on your blocklist stays blocked throughout the timer, so you won't be tempted to "just quickly check" something.

## Timer Notifications

VisionFocus keeps you informed throughout your focus session without requiring you to watch the timer.

### Notification Types

- **Session started** — Confirms your focus session has begun
- **Session complete** — Alerts you when the timer reaches zero
- **Break reminder** — Prompts you to rest before starting the next session

### Managing Notifications

1. Open VisionFocus settings
2. Navigate to **Notifications**
3. Toggle each notification type on or off as needed

> **Important:** Make sure Chrome notifications are enabled in your system settings. On macOS, go to **System Settings → Notifications → Google Chrome**. On Windows, go to **Settings → System → Notifications → Google Chrome**.

## Focus Tracking and Statistics

VisionFocus records your browsing behavior during timer sessions, giving you data to understand and improve your focus quality.

### What Gets Tracked

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Session time | Total time spent in active focus sessions |
| Sites visited | Which sites you navigated to during the timer |
| Distraction attempts | Number of times you tried to visit a blocked site |

### Reviewing Your Stats

1. Complete a timer session
2. Open the **Stats** tab in VisionFocus
3. Review your focus metrics
4. Look for patterns — which sessions went well? What interrupted you?

Use this data to adjust your blocklist, timer duration, or work environment.

## Combining Timers with Website Blocking

For the highest level of focus, run the timer and blocking features together:

1. Add your most distracting sites to the [blocklist](/en/docs/vision-focus/blocking-websites/)
2. Start your focus timer
3. Work — any attempt to visit a blocked site will be intercepted during the session

This combination removes the willpower required to resist distractions. Instead of deciding each time whether to visit a site, the decision is already made for the duration of the session.

## Pausing and Resetting

- **Pause**: You can pause the timer at any time. Your progress is saved and the timer picks up where it left off when you resume.
- **Reset**: Resetting returns the timer to your chosen duration without saving the partial session.

## Troubleshooting Timer Issues

### Timer doesn't start

- Confirm VisionFocus is enabled at `chrome://extensions/`
- Click the VisionFocus icon to reload the popup
- Restart Chrome if the issue persists

### Notifications aren't arriving

- Check that Chrome notifications are enabled: `chrome://settings/content/notifications`
- Make sure your OS notification settings allow Chrome to send notifications
- Verify that Do Not Disturb mode is off

### Timer stops unexpectedly in the background

This is a known Chrome limitation related to how extensions handle background timers. To work around it:
- Keep at least one Chrome window open during a session
- Make sure your computer doesn't go to sleep while the timer is running
- Update VisionFocus to the latest version, as background timer handling improves with each release
