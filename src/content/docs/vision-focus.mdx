# VisionFocus

**Focus on what matters.** VisionFocus is a Chrome extension that helps you block distracting websites, protect your vision with timers, and achieve your goals with daily vision statements.

## Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="🎯 Website Blocking" icon="seti:lock">
		Block distracting websites with a single click. Customize your blocklist and stay focused on your work.
	</Card>
	<Card title="👁️ Vision Protection" icon="seti:clock">
		Set timers to remind yourself to take breaks. Protect your eyes and maintain healthy browsing habits.
	</Card>
	<Card title="🌟 Daily Vision" icon="seti:notebook">
		Write and display your daily vision statement. Keep your goals visible and stay motivated throughout the day.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
